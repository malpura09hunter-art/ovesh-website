QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only work with businesses, or individuals too?

Both. Services scale from individual website security reviews to full business engagements like penetration testing and ongoing consulting.

Is penetration testing legal?

Yes — all testing is conducted only on systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test, following responsible, ethical practices.

How long does a typical engagement take?

It depends on scope — a focused vulnerability assessment might take days, while a full web build or in-depth pentest can take several weeks. You'll get a timeline in the proposal.

Can you fix issues you find, not just report them?

Yes — remediation support and secure re-development are both available as part of or after an assessment.

How do we get started?

Reach out through the contact section with a brief description of what you need — you'll hear back to schedule a discovery call.
