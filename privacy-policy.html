01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ovesh Malpura Cyber Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the website at malpuraovesh.vercel.app and its Client Portal.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably needed to operate the website, provide account functionality, communicate with users, and maintain security.

02Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the website may process:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, authentication provider, and account timestamps when you create or use a Client Portal account.
  • Authentication information: information required by our authentication provider to sign you in. We do not receive or store your Google or Microsoft account password.
  • Account activity: information such as account creation time and last-login time for account management and security.
  • Security information: information supplied to our login-security endpoint, which may include your account email, display name, and browser user-agent information when a login alert is generated.
  • Information you voluntarily provide: information you choose to send when contacting us or requesting services.

03Security Tools on This Website

The Password Checker and Phone Security Analyzer on the main website are designed to operate in your browser. The password checker is not intended to transmit, store, or access the password you enter on our server. The phone security analyzer uses answers you provide to produce an educational result and does not access your device, contacts, files, or hardware.

You should never enter a real password that you use for another account into a security demonstration or testing tool.

04How We Use Information

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain Client Portal accounts.
  • To provide requested client or website services.
  • To send account-related messages such as verification, welcome, password-reset, or security notifications where applicable.
  • To record limited account activity needed for security and account management.
  • To respond to communications and service requests.
  • To detect, investigate, and help prevent misuse, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • To maintain and improve the website and its functionality.

05Authentication and Third-Party Services

The Client Portal uses Firebase Authentication and related Firebase services to support account authentication and account data. Google and Microsoft sign-in may be available as authentication options. When you choose an external sign-in provider, that provider processes information according to its own privacy practices.

The website is hosted through Vercel. Certain transactional or account-related backend services may also process information when those features are enabled.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

06Cookies, Local Storage, and Similar Technologies

The Client Portal may use browser storage, authentication cookies, or similar technologies to maintain a signed-in session and remember necessary authentication state. These technologies are used to provide requested functionality and security.

You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings, although disabling required storage may prevent parts of the Client Portal from working correctly.

07Data Sharing

We may share or permit access to information with service providers that are necessary to operate the website, authentication, hosting, email, storage, or security functions. Such providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of our services.

08Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing accounts and services, maintaining security records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or operational requirements.

Retention periods can vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

09Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10Your Choices and Requests

Where applicable, you may contact us to request information about personal data associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of account information, subject to legitimate security, legal, and operational requirements.

To make a privacy request, contact info.oveshmalpuracyberlab@zohomail.in.

11Children's Privacy

Our services are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
