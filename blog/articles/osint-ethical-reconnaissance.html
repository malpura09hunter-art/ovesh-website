OSINT 101: Ethical Reconnaissance Techniques

By Ovesh Malpura · Educational article

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) is the structured collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources. Good OSINT work is scoped, documented and careful about privacy and attribution.

OSINT fundamentals

Write down the purpose, scope, permitted sources and stopping conditions before collecting information. A focused question reduces unnecessary collection.

Practical steps

Prefer original public records, official websites, direct statements and source documents. Record access dates and enough context to verify a claim.

Corroborate important findings with independent sources. Separate direct evidence from interpretation and communicate uncertainty honestly.

Key takeaways

Do not publish sensitive personal details, attempt unauthorised access, evade privacy controls or harass individuals. Public availability does not automatically make every use appropriate.
