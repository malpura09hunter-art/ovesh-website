1. Scope

Services follow the accepted request or later-approved quotation. Work outside the agreed scope may require a separate quote.

Shop prices may be estimates or starting prices. Final pricing and payment terms are confirmed in the applicable quotation or invoice.

4. Delivery

Timelines are estimates and depend on scope, feedback, approvals and third-party services.

Domains, hosting, APIs, software subscriptions, AI providers and other third-party services may have separate fees and terms.

After agreed payments are completed, deliverables are provided as specified in the quotation. Third-party components remain subject to their own licenses.

7. Security & acceptable use

Cybersecurity work is performed only with authorized scope and permission. Unlawful access, credential theft, malware deployment and unauthorized testing are not included.

Cancellation and refund terms depend on project stage and the applicable quotation or invoice. Completed work and non-refundable third-party costs may not be refundable.

9. Support

Post-delivery support is included only when stated in the quotation. New features and work outside included support are additional.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties should protect non-public business, technical and project information, subject to legal requirements and necessary service providers.

11. Acceptance

Submitting a request confirms review of these terms. A request is not itself final acceptance of a quotation or a guarantee that the project will be accepted.
