DECODED.

This chapter turns the luxury object into a Life Science story — focusing on Vitamin C, antioxidant chemistry, formulation stability and responsible skincare communication.

C
01

Antioxidant role

Vitamin C is the central antioxidant ingredient explored by the OVESH concept. Antioxidants are studied for their ability to interact with reactive species.

02

Stability matters

Vitamin C formulations require thoughtful attention to factors such as oxidation, light, air, water activity, pH and packaging.

03

The whole system

A formula is more than one active. Supporting ingredients, compatibility, delivery system and packaging all contribute to the product concept.

ACADEMIC NOTE

Science first.
Claims second.

The OVESH website is designed to explain a scientific idea without turning design language into unsupported medical or clinical claims.

The project therefore avoids invented clinical percentages, before-and-after promises, fake certifications, fake reviews and made-up test results. The goal is to show how a skincare product can be communicated through formulation science and visual design.
