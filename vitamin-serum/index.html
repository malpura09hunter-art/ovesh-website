03 / THE SCIENCE

THE SCIENCE
BEHIND THE GLOW.

Vitamin C is a small molecule with a big place in skincare research. The story starts with antioxidant chemistry — and ends with responsible communication.

C₆H₈O₆
01

WHAT IS VITAMIN C?

Vitamin C is a water-soluble vitamin and antioxidant. In skincare, topical Vitamin C is used in formulations designed around antioxidant and appearance-related benefits.

02

WHY ANTIOXIDANTS?

Antioxidants can participate in chemical processes that help manage reactive molecules. This is why antioxidant ingredients are important subjects in skincare formulation.

03

WHY STABILITY MATTERS

Vitamin C can be sensitive to environmental conditions such as oxygen, light and heat. Formulation and packaging choices therefore matter when developing active-ingredient products.
