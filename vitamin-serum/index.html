01 / THE PRODUCT

Designed for
discovery.

OVESH Serum is a student Life Science concept presented like a premium skincare launch — visually bold, but scientifically responsible.

The concept explores how Vitamin C and complementary antioxidant and conditioning ingredients can be communicated through a modern formulation story. The packaging world is intentionally luxurious, while the science sections explain the role of each ingredient without unsupported clinical promises.
