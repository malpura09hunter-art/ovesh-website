OVESH SERUM keeps the first impression deliberately simple: one sealed bottle, one identity, one scientific focus.

The visual language uses dark materials, champagne-gold details and controlled light to communicate precision. It is a design concept for an academic Life Science project, not a claim of commercial manufacturing.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Sealed by design

The closed cap and sealed presentation reinforce product integrity as a visual idea and keep the hero object unmistakably closed.

02

Night radiance

A concentrated halo creates the cinematic glow around the bottle without making unrealistic clinical promises.

03

Scientific restraint

Formula details are presented as educational storytelling rather than invented percentages, test results or certifications.

04

OVESH identity

The product name remains consistent across the bottle, packaging, navigation and chapter system.
